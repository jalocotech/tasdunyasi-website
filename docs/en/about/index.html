About Us

Natural stone, mining, landscaping and construction since 1973

Taş Dünyası is part of a group of family-owned companies. Over more than 40 years of trading, our partners have built experience in mining, natural stone, landscaping and construction, and a valued circle of customers and suppliers.

Since 2003 we have operated at the same address in Kemerburgaz, Istanbul, on 10,000 m² of open and covered area next to the historic Uzun Kemer, the aqueduct given its present form by Mimar Sinan. With our natural stone and glass tumbling plants, our one-off art nouveau approach and more than 500 product varieties, we are unique in Istanbul.

History

A journey of nearly half a century

  1. 1973

    Foundations laid

    The foundations of the group were laid by the same partners of today with the sale of construction materials. They also worked as subcontractors on the construction of the Kemer Country villas in Kemerburgaz.

  2. 1992

    Stone quarry

    Quarry operations for aggregate production began on a licensed site.

  3. 2003

    Taş Dünyası

    Natural stone trading began. Taş Dünyası has operated at the same address in Kemerburgaz ever since.

  4. 2016

    Construction

    In June 2016 the first housing project, with 24 apartments and 4 shops, was started.

  5. 2017

    A new era

    Yakut Evleri (Arnavutköy / Bolluca) was delivered in July. Taş Dünyası's licensed quarry site was put into operation under a royalty (rödovans) agreement.

Our Mission

Keeping trust, adding value

  • To preserve the trust placed in the natural stone, glass, stone-wood-metal art nouveau sculptures and reliefs, and antiques we sell for construction, landscaping and decoration, and to add value to our customers' lives with our products.
  • To carry out our work in line with the law, industry practice and social customs, and open to innovation.
  • To put customer satisfaction first through fast, uninterrupted service, supplying the tonnage requested.
  • To take all necessary measures for the safety of our employees so that they work in a healthier and safer environment.
Our Vision

The first name in stone

  • To serve the bringing of our country's natural stones into the economy and to add health and value to people's lives.
  • To satisfy customers with quality standards, fair prices, and uninterrupted supply guaranteed in the tonnage requested.
  • To source high-quality, highly varied products from suppliers at fair prices and offer them to customers.
  • To be open to innovation and creative, to keep the widest variety under one roof, and to remain a company with high brand awareness and reputation through our natural stone and glass tumbling plant.
  • To remain one of the first companies that come to mind for tumbled natural stone, glass, hand-made art nouveau sculptures, reliefs, functional landscape products and antiques.
What we do

What do we do?

We supply products from every region of Turkey and from suppliers in other countries, for use in construction, landscaping, indoor and outdoor decoration, paving and cladding.

01

Natural stones

Untumbled or sized natural stones in great variety, and natural rocks in different sizes.

02

Landscape glass

Untumbled landscape and decoration glass in various sizes, including rock-form pieces.

03

Our own tumbling plant

Natural stone and glass tumbled in our own facilities, in different sizes and varieties.

04

Antique stones and amphorae

Used old and antique stones, and amphorae.

05

Hand-made sculptures and reliefs

Made by the sculptors on our staff from natural stone, tree roots, solid logs and metal: one-off works with corporate lettering, logos, portraits, animals, flowers, objects and abstract themes, never repeated.

06

Functional landscape products

Functional and creative products such as garden seating sets, benches, planters, flower stands, fountains, pools and sprays.

07

Antiques

An antique collection of more than 400 varieties, mostly bought at auctions.

Our commitment to customers: uninterrupted supply at consistent quality, a contract price that does not change, and a guarantee of supply in high tonnage. Taş Dünyası does not carry out installation; on request, we refer you to the installers and tradespeople we have worked with for years.
